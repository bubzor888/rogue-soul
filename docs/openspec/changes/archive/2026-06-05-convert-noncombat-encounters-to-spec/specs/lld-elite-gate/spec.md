## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: [LLD-EG-001] Elite Gate Structure
The Elite Gate SHALL present a two-door choice: one door showing an elite combat encounter, one door showing an Anomaly encounter. Both doors display their full identity before the player commits. This is a forced structural beat — not drawn from the general room pool. See `LLD-FLOOR-BEATS-004` (floor encounter design) for placement.

#### Scenario: Both doors identified
- **WHEN** the player reaches the Elite Gate room slot
- **THEN** one door shows the specific elite enemy identity and one door shows the Anomaly symbol; the player knows exactly what each contains before choosing

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EG-002] Elite Door Rewards
Completing the elite combat SHALL provide:
- One guaranteed elite-tier item (drawn from a pool unique to elite encounters or a higher-tier drop pool)
- One standard consumable drop
- A **fixed, significant HP restoration** after the fight — the same amount on every elite encounter across runs

`[OPEN]` Fixed HP restoration amount — to be tuned once vessel HP pools and typical elite combat damage values are established. Design intent: restores roughly what a competent run of the elite costs, leaving the player near their pre-fight HP.

#### Scenario: Post-elite HP restoration
- **WHEN** the player completes the elite combat
- **THEN** a fixed HP amount is restored; this restoration is displayed consistently so experienced players can factor it into their door decision

#### Scenario: Elite item tier
- **WHEN** the player receives post-elite loot
- **THEN** the main item reward is elite-tier — higher quality than a standard post-combat drop

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EG-003] Standard Door Rewards
The Anomaly door at the Elite Gate is a risk/reward alternative to the known elite fight. Outcomes are generated by `EncounterFactory.build_anomaly()` and may be beneficial, harmful, or neutral. No post-fight healing.

#### Scenario: Anomaly outcome variance
- **WHEN** the player takes the Anomaly door at the Elite Gate
- **THEN** the outcome is unknown until resolution — it could provide a meaningful advantage or impose a meaningful cost

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EG-004] Elite Gate is the Only Post-Fight Heal
The fixed HP restoration after the elite fight is the ONLY guaranteed post-fight healing on the floor. There is no guaranteed rest room at any point on Floor 3. Healing outside the elite fight is only available if the player encounters a Wandering Soul item-for-HP trade through normal room generation.

#### Scenario: No other post-fight healing
- **WHEN** the player completes any standard combat encounter (not the elite)
- **THEN** no HP is restored; the player exits with the same HP they had when the fight ended

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EG-005] Elite vs Standard Enemy Distinction
`[OPEN]` Whether the standard (Anomaly) door at the Elite Gate is always a different enemy type from the elite door — or whether it can be the same enemy at a lower tier — is unresolved. Current lean: yes, always distinct, so the choice presents two clearly different propositions.

#### Scenario: [OPEN] Door enemy distinction
- **WHEN** the Elite Gate is generated
- **THEN** a decision must be made whether the two doors always show distinct enemy types
