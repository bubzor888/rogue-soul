## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: [LLD-NAR-001] Guardian Dialogue — Per Vessel
`[OPEN·MVP1]` The Judge SHALL deliver dialogue specific to the vessel petitioning for entry. Dialogue SHALL reflect what the guardian observes in that soul — what it still carries, what it has lost, and whether those qualities justify passage or refusal. Dialogue is written per vessel in a dedicated narrative writing session (see `HLD-NAR-002` for the guardian's test mechanic).

#### Scenario: [OPEN·MVP1] Guardian dialogue written for Pilgrim
- **WHEN** the Pilgrim faces the Judge
- **THEN** the Judge's dialogue acknowledges the Pilgrim's emptiness as the qualifying condition — not as pity, but as a neutral recognition of need

---

### Requirement: [LLD-NAR-002] Branch Endings
`[OPEN·MVP2]` Each branch of the vessel tree SHALL produce a distinct ending reflecting what that soul carried to Solace and what the guardian saw in them. The destination — Solace — is the same for all vessels. The path, the guardian's judgment, and what the soul finds there differ. Ending content is written per branch in a dedicated narrative writing session.

`[OPEN·MVP2]` Whether the player eventually learns the full shape of the soul's history across branches, or whether each branch remains its own complete story, to be decided during narrative design.

#### Scenario: [OPEN·MVP2] Endings differ by branch
- **WHEN** the player completes a run with different vessels
- **THEN** the ending content (guardian's judgment, Solace arrival) is distinct per vessel branch

---

### Requirement: [LLD-NAR-003] Vessel Lore Fragments
`[OPEN·MVP1]` Each vessel SHALL have associated lore fragments that the Soul Codex accumulates across runs. Fragments surface the vessel's history — their circumstances of death, their relationship to Solace, glimpses of the lives that came before them in the erosion path. Fragment content is written per vessel in a dedicated writing session.

#### Scenario: [OPEN·MVP1] Pilgrim lore fragments written
- **WHEN** the player encounters a Memory Fragment while playing the Pilgrim
- **THEN** the narrative content belongs to the Pilgrim's lore pool (or a past vessel's pool, hinting at lives not yet unlocked)

---

### Requirement: [LLD-NAR-004] Floor Lore — Tier 2 Floors
`[OPEN·MVP3]` The Blurred Deep (Hedge Knight's floor) and the Unmarked Edge (Drifter's floor) SHALL each have a defined set of lore content — environmental descriptions, enemy framing, and narrative callbacks to the vessels whose erosion paths pass through them. Content written in a dedicated narrative session once Tier 2 vessels are fully designed.

#### Scenario: [OPEN·MVP3] Blurred Deep atmosphere defined
- **WHEN** a Tier 2 or Tier 3 vessel on the solo path plays Floor 2
- **THEN** the floor presents as the Blurred Deep — underground, crypt and cave geometry bleeding together, undead and elemental enemies
