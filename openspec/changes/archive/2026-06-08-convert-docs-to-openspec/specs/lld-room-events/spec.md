## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: [LLD-EVENTS-001] Memory Fragment Rooms
Memory Fragment rooms SHALL present a narrative/lore event in which the soul recovers a piece of its own history. Each fragment is recorded in the Soul Codex (see HLD-META-002). Fragment content is seeded from `EncounterFactory` using the room's seed_offset.

#### Scenario: Fragment recorded in Codex
- **WHEN** a player completes a Memory Fragment room
- **THEN** a new Codex entry is created and the SignalBus.codex_entry_added signal is emitted

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EVENTS-002] Wandering Soul Rooms
Wandering Soul rooms SHALL present a merchant-equivalent encounter — a lost spirit that remembers what it had in life. The player may trade items. Inventory is generated by `EncounterFactory.build_wandering_soul()` using the floor's item pools.

#### Scenario: Wandering Soul inventory generation
- **WHEN** a Wandering Soul room is entered
- **THEN** a set of items from the floor's pools is presented; the player may acquire one or more via the trade mechanic (trade rules `[OPEN]`)

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EVENTS-003] Rest / Mending Rooms
Rest rooms SHALL allow the player to heal the vessel or restore the bound companion. Framing is vessel-repair, not a campfire. Triggers the `"rest_room"` replenishment event (see HLD-ARCH-011), restoring charges for any ability or item with `rest_room` in its replenish_triggers.

#### Scenario: Vessel heal
- **WHEN** the player chooses to heal the vessel in a Rest room
- **THEN** vessel HP is restored by a defined amount (value `[OPEN]`)

#### Scenario: Companion restore
- **WHEN** the bound companion is alive and the player chooses companion restore in a Rest room
- **THEN** companion HP is restored by a defined amount (value `[OPEN]`)

#### Scenario: Replenishment event
- **WHEN** a Rest room is completed
- **THEN** RunController fires the "rest_room" replenishment event and ChargeManager restores matching charges

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EVENTS-004] Anomaly Rooms
Anomaly rooms SHALL have an unknown/corrupted symbol and an unknown outcome. They are a risk/reward room — the outcome is generated by `EncounterFactory.build_anomaly()` and may be beneficial, harmful, or neutral.

#### Scenario: Unknown outcome on entry
- **WHEN** a player selects an Anomaly door
- **THEN** the room type is not revealed until the player enters; the door symbol is visually corrupted/unreadable (see HLD-RUN-002)

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EVENTS-005] Echo Chamber Rooms
Echo Chamber rooms SHALL contain an encounter with a remnant of a past vessel or enemy encountered in previous runs. Entities known to the Soul Codex grant the in-run bonus from HLD-META-002.

#### Scenario: Known entity bonus
- **WHEN** the player enters an Echo Chamber and the entity is in their Soul Codex
- **THEN** the Codex bonus for that entity applies to the encounter

---

### Requirement: [LLD-EVENTS-006] Omen System (Full Design Deferred)
The full omen cycle mechanics are documented in `docs/detailed design/soul_protocol_omens.md`. Key integration points with other systems:
- Combat encounters use the omen deck for status effect timing (see HLD-COMBAT-006 and HLD-COMBAT-008)
- Each enemy contributes omen cards to the deck for the duration of their combat (see LLD-ENEMIES-003 and individual enemy requirements)
- Omen deck composition is seeded via the COMBAT RNG stream (see HLD-ARCH-008)

`[OPEN]` Full omen requirements to be converted to spec format in a dedicated follow-on change once the omens doc is reviewed.

#### Scenario: [OPEN] Omen deck spec conversion
- **WHEN** the omen spec conversion is completed
- **THEN** all omen card types, deck reset rules, and player steering mechanics are captured as LLD-EVENTS requirements
